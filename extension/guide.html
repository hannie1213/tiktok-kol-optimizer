Lumio Creator Capture

Adds the TikTok creators you browse to your Lumio creator pool, so your team can find and shortlist them.

把你浏览过的 TikTok 达人自动加入 Lumio 达人池，方便团队查找和筛选。

Connect in 30 seconds30 秒完成连接

  1. Pin the extension固定扩展 Click the puzzle icon in the browser toolbar and pin Lumio. 点浏览器工具栏上的拼图图标，把 Lumio 固定到工具栏。
  2. Create a connection code生成连接码 Sign in to your Lumio site, open Explore, click Browser extension, then Create connection code. It is copied for you. 登录 Lumio 网站，打开「发现达人」，点「浏览器扩展」，再点「生成连接码」，连接码会自动复制。
  3. Paste it粘贴连接码 Click the Lumio icon and paste. It connects on its own and shows your email. 点 Lumio 图标，把连接码粘贴进去，会自动连接并显示你的邮箱。

Everyday use日常使用

Automatic: open any creator profile (tiktok.com/@name). The public handle, follower and like counts are sent to Lumio and a note appears in the corner. The same creator is not re-sent within 6 hours.

自动采集：打开任意达人主页（tiktok.com/@账号），公开的账号、粉丝数和获赞数会发送到 Lumio，页面右下角会弹出提示。同一位达人 6 小时内不会重复发送。

Manual: on a profile, click the Lumio icon and press Capture this page.

手动采集：在达人主页点 Lumio 图标，再点「采集当前页面」。

Find them: captured creators appear on Explore in Lumio for your whole team. Add the ones you like to your Creators library.

在哪里看：采集到的达人会出现在 Lumio 的「发现达人」，整个团队都能看到。看中的可以加入「达人库」。

✓Sent to the pool已发送到达人池
!Code expired or capture failed. Open the pop-up.连接码失效或采集失败，点开弹窗处理

FAQ常见问题

Nothing is captured打开主页后没有采集

Make sure the address is a profile (tiktok.com/@name), not a video or the For You feed. Refresh TikTok tabs that were open before installing. Check that the pop-up says Connected and automatic capture is on.

确认网址是达人主页（tiktok.com/@账号），视频页和推荐页不会采集。安装前就打开的 TikTok 页面需要刷新一次。再看弹窗里是否显示「已连接」、自动采集是否打开。

The icon shows a red “!”图标上出现红色「!」

The code expired (for example after a password change). Click Reconnect in the pop-up and paste a new code. Captures made meanwhile are kept and sent afterwards.

连接码失效了（比如修改过密码）。在弹窗里点「重新连接」，粘贴新的连接码即可。这期间采集的达人会保留，之后自动补发。

Does it read my account?会读取我的账号吗？

No. It only reads public numbers on creator profiles and only talks to your Lumio site.

不会。只读取达人主页上公开的数字，并且只和你的 Lumio 网站通信。

How do I update?怎么更新？

Download the extension again from Lumio, replace this folder with the new one, then click reload on the Lumio card in chrome://extensions.

在 Lumio 网站重新下载，用新文件夹替换旧的，再到 chrome://extensions 里点 Lumio 卡片上的刷新按钮。
