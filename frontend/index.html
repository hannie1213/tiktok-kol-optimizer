Grow the pool with the browser extension

                
With the extension installed, every creator profile you or your team open is added to the pool automatically. It reads only what the public profile page shows.

                
1
Download and unzipA small Chrome / Edge extension.

                
2
Load it in your browserOpen chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped tiko-capture folder.

                
3
Connect it to your accountCreate a connection code, then paste it into the extension's pop-up. The code can only submit creators — it cannot open your workspace.
                    

                    

                
4
Browse as usualOpen creator profiles; they appear here within seconds. Changing your password disconnects the extension.
